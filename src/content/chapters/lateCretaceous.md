This was the pack has two firsts for a Frontier trailer: First person camera shots and Nvidia's Ansel game filters.

The former was due to me adapting the inclusion of a 'Guest POV' mode as part of the free update tied to this pack and adjusting the parameters to allow for greater focal lengths, smoothing, and placing them inside the exhibits. Meanwhile the Ansel game filters used the depth information from the game for realtime depth of field. The combination of these two elements balanced out well, the mouse controlled camera giving a tactile handheld motion to the camera, while the filters gave a more cinematic feel.

Squeezed inbetween two larger story DLCs this was smaller in scope and timeframe.
